## Search record and negative evidence

Queries included: tensor-train honest inference; tensor confidence intervals local modulus; higher-order local modulus polynomial inference; product-of-coefficients uniform confidence intervals; first-order degeneracy 2026; Segre–Veronese osculating-space formula. Exact source identifiers were opened and the named results located. No directly matching capped-TT loading-uniform modulus and honest-length statement was found in these retrieved sources. Sparse search results for this terminology do not exclude work under algebraic statistics, optimal recovery, product-mean inference, or different tensor formats.

**Novelty decision:** the new multiscale statement is a proved strengthening of this project's original theorem, not a certified improvement over a named prior theorem under the same assumptions. The signed-path argument is elementary and may still be regarded as a useful application of known product geometry. This concern is retained in the final publication assessment. Generic confidence-region inversion, simultaneous contrasts, Markov localization and inverse-Wishart moment sharpening are supporting improvements, not independent publication-level novelty claims.

## Audit completion: robust localization and the variance-bound obstruction

The final matrix has 28 rows. [Devroye et al. (2016)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.05845) and [Lugosi–Mendelson (2019)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.00482) establish robust mean-estimation principles well beyond the crude coordinate amplification used here. The latter's bound separates trace and largest-eigenvalue terms; our fixed-dimensional certificate does not improve it. Its mathematical proof does not rely on an unstated robust-estimation theorem.
