Audit addendum: independent finite-variance noise
A final study, specified after the proof of Theorem 12, uses independent Gaussian designs, B0 =
3E0000, A = E1111, and a known variance bound ¯σ2 = 0.22. The errors are either Gaussian or
standardized Student t2.5; the latter have finite variance but no finite third or fourth moment. Each
noise/sample-size cohort contains 1,000 datasets at n = 512, 2048, 8192, and the same observations
are used for the q = 3 and q = 4 cap profiles. Thus there are 6,000 additional datasets and 12,000
intervals.
Every median-localized interval covers in these runs; the Wilson interval for 1,000 successes is
[0.9962, 1]. Under t2.5 noise the fitted mean-length slopes are −1.598 and −2.133 for q = 3, 4, with
Monte Carlo standard errors 0.0010 and 0.0015. These slopes remain steeper than −1.5 and −2
over this finite grid and are not presented as precise asymptotic confirmation. Figure 5 displays the
finite-sample behavior. The deliberately large variance-bound radius explains the conservatism; the
result establishes a proved order under weaker noise assumptions, not a practically efficient robust
interval.
