The roles of the assumptions are distinct. Original-mode rank one makes the within-block
witness paths feasible with internal ranks at most two. The known cap-one cuts exclude the
ambient directions responsible for slower inference. The norm cap provides compactness and an
interior margin; Corollary 11 removes it. Gaussian errors give exact chi-square and F localization;
isotropic random design gives the stated information distance and length moments. Corollary 9
proves the same rate under explicit more general design conditions. Proposition 10 gives a separate
conditional-moment noise result with a slower upper bound; Theorem 12 restores the full rate using
independent groups and a known variance bound. Neither extension implies unrestricted observation
contamination, arbitrary dependent-noise validity, or growing-dimensional optimality.
