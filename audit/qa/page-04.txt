Finite-variance localization.
Robust mean estimation with finite second moments is developed
by Devroye et al. (2016); Lugosi and Mendelson (2019). Our coordinate-median OLS argument uses
the classical independent-group amplification principle, then transfers the resulting radius moments
through the TT modulus. It does not improve the dimension or confidence-level dependence of
those mean-estimation results. The need to restrict a fully nonparametric unknown-noise class is
consistent with the impossibility literature originating with Bahadur and Savage (1956); the explicit
regression construction in Appendix C is proved directly.
Modern tensor models and other inferential targets.
Tensor regression networks (Kossaifi
et al., 2020), global rank-adaptive TT adapters (Lopez-Piqueres et al., 2026), and statistical rank
allocation for LoRA (Gao and Tan, 2026) motivate attention to rank restrictions. Their architectural
or optimization targets should not be conflated with the present population coefficient. Tensor
conformal completion (Sun and Chen, 2026) and the recent dual-channel tensor framework (Chen
et al., 2026) further demonstrate that tensor uncertainty quantification is already an active area.
Predictive intervals and structure-comparison guarantees in those experiments do not determine the
honest length studied here.
