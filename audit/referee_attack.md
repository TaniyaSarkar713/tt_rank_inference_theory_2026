## Efficiency, bootstrap and computational questions

In a smooth unrestricted block-product submodel under isotropic Gaussian regression, the first-order functional variance is sigma²||Π_{≤1}A||²/n. At an exactly normal loading this vanishes, so ordinary root-n efficiency language does not describe the higher-order target. At internally singular capped-TT points, the smooth-envelope tangent space must not be silently identified with the constrained local experiment. No efficient constant is proved for the proposed intervals; the simulations demonstrate large constants even after the energy improvement.

No bootstrap procedure is used. C10 proves a simple ordinary-bootstrap failure under degeneracy. A generic bootstrap replacement would require a separate conditional-law argument and cannot be presumed valid from smoothness alone.

HOSVD is computed spectrally, with a proved quasioptimal residual; no optimization landscape, local minimum or oracle starting point is hidden. Median OLS fits g observable group regressions. The code enumerates 2^J masks for general loadings. A better algorithm or localizer would need its own guaranteed approximation error incorporated in the deterministic certificate. Exact local injective-norm hardness does not prove hardness of rate-optimal honest intervals.

The fixed-group median procedure is not robust to a fixed fraction of arbitrary replacements. One corrupted response in each of h groups can drive a designated OLS coordinate arbitrarily far for a generic fixed design, and hence move its median; its replacement breakdown fraction is at most h/n. This tends to zero with fixed J. The finite-variance iid theorem is therefore not an adversarial-contamination theorem. Huber-mixture lower bounds, positive contamination tolerance and optimal contamination exponents remain unproved.

The known variance-bound assumption in the broad conditional-moment class cannot be removed for free: C12 proves nonshrinking expected length when honesty ranges over every finite bound. Gaussian unknown variance is allowed because it imposes a much smaller noise family. An unknown-scale extension needs explicit additional tail/shape assumptions, not merely a residual estimator.
