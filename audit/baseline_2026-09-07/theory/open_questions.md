# Unresolved directions

## O1 — PARTIAL: dimensions increasing
The present exact conditional coverage is valid at any full-rank design, but the rate theorem fixes p. Determine matching upper/lower bounds exposing block dimensions, loadings and internal caps as p increases. An ambient confidence ball can have very large constants. No dimension-free n^(-q/2) theorem follows from this draft. A meaningful next result would use an intrinsic, honest localization region and prove matching loading-dependent lower bounds.

## O2 — CONJECTURED: non-rank-one points and weak bonds
For arbitrary B0 of TT rank r below some caps, characterize the smallest order of a feasible analytic arc with nonzero target derivative. Multiple weak bonds may interact. Existing tangent and second-order tangent results are necessary prior art; they do not automatically supply a uniform statistical modulus. No universal exponent or transition formula is asserted here. A counterexample to an oversimplified rule should be retained as a contribution.

## O3 — PARTIAL: computation and constants
The injective-norm reduction concerns exact local constants, not interval computation or minimax risk. Establishing a statistical-computational gap would require a reduction with a precise coverage/length decision task. No such proof exists in this project.

## O4 — CONJECTURED: noise and design generalizations
Conditional coverage survives arbitrary full-rank design with iid homoskedastic Gaussian errors. It does not automatically survive heavy tails, heteroskedasticity or dependence. Honest robust localization could replace the Gaussian ball; proving its radius moments and sharp induced functional length remains work.

## O5 — CONJECTURED: data-dependent caps or estimated loadings
A rank pretest cannot certify an approximation tolerance below the ambient noise scale uniformly. External structural restrictions, separate validation assumptions, or explicit sensitivity analysis are needed. For loadings learned from the same observations, additional selection uncertainty must be handled.
