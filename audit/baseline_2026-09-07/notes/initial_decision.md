# Initial skeptical decision

Decision on the preceding four-way quadratic/cubic paper: **Incremental — reformulate the contribution.**

The main novelty threats are specific. Chen–Fang (2019) already treats arbitrary-order delta expansions; Bei–Navjeevan (2026) gives uniform inference near degeneracy and explicitly discusses higher-order cases; Pillai–Meng (2016) provides exact Gaussian monomial Wald limits. The preceding class is a smooth grouped rank-one model, so its cubic rate does not by itself establish a new TT singularity phenomenon. Existing TT tangent-cone and second-order intersection geometry also preclude claiming new general geometric machinery.

Revision to investigate before experiments:
1. Classify the local-modulus exponent for every nonzero loading at a globally rank-one tensor under arbitrary positive integer TT rank caps.
2. Show how the classification depends on cuts whose upper rank is one, even with genuinely singular internal constraints.
3. Prove the sharp loss from admitting a known Frobenius tube around the exact rank class.
4. Build a computable honest interval attaining the resulting rates, allowing unknown Gaussian noise variance.
5. Separate exact statistical rates from the computational difficulty of sharp modulus constants.

Candidate significance: a general classification and an explicit structural-tolerance threshold. Generic higher-order inference, projection intervals, tube-modulus inequalities and tensor-norm hardness are antecedents, not new principles.

No claim of a paradigm shift is justified.
