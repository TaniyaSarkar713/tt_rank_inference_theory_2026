Six-paragraph introduction outline:
1. Explain functional uncertainty in a structured coefficient; use the four-way cubic contrast as the running example. No unmet input requirement.
2. Identify at most three existing limitations: nondegenerate alignment conditions; generic rather than TT-specific higher-order theory; lack of a supplied approximation certificate. Do not imply prior work lacks all inference.
3. Define the actual question as honest expected length at a rank-one point over the full capped class. Fixed dimensions and exact Gaussian experiment are explicit.
4. Explain the three challenges in the table. A single matrix bound can lose the exponent; a generic delta calculation does not certify honesty.
5. Explain cap-one blocks, feasible rank-two paths, and graded confidence-ball inversion. Return to the cubic example and the six-way internal singularity.
6. Give three contributions linked to theory/method sections. The complexity observation is secondary, not a fourth major novelty claim.
