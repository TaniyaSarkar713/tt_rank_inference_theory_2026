## Proof checkpoints and attempted failure modes

1. **Grouping relaxes the class.** Retaining strong cuts gives an upper bound only. The lower construction explicitly retains every original core size; it does not transplant an infeasible grouped perturbation.
2. **Grouped physical normality.** Each grouped loading contracts at least one original normal mode. It therefore annihilates the reference even when the within-block ranks are not controlled by the relaxed model.
3. **The lower bound is valid beyond the smallest gap.** Core amplitudes before the last site are capped at their own $\beta_j$. The last-site amplitude is proportional to $t$. Orthogonal expansion by changed physical sites bounds the full Frobenius norm at every radius. Merely using an equal small radius at all sites would not establish the claimed transition.
4. **Right-interface normalization.** Left-orthonormal cores give $\|H_jR_j\|_F=1$ and $\|H_j\|_{\mathrm{op}}=1/\beta_j$. These are different facts and play different roles in the last-site and earlier-site estimates.
5. **Boundary density is not assumed silently.** The theorem states complementary-space admissibility. Interpolating padded normal cores toward one exact-rank tuple and avoiding finitely many polynomial roots proves the required density. No boundary claim without this premise is made.
6. **Singular spectra are not arbitrary parameters.** The rate formula evaluates spectra of actual feasible tensors. The explicit example separately computes its three spectra and supplies a witness at its exact rank loss.
7. **Expected length includes rare outcomes.** The inequality $F(st)\le\max(s,s^K)F(t)$ uses the existing first and Kth OLS moments. It replaces the earlier small-gap fallback conversion that introduced the balance constant.
8. **Honesty and short length have different scopes.** The 23-feature interval is honest for every original rank-capped tensor; its length analysis uses the displayed family, where all nonzero signal coefficients are selected.
9. **Omitted signal is retained in the variance.** Selected regression uses $\tau_B^2=\sigma^2+\|B_{\mathcal F^c}\|^2$ and estimates it from residuals. The equality $\tau_B=\sigma$ is used only at the reference family.
10. **Both cross bounds share one coverage event.** No rank pretest or extra union-bound allocation is needed. The grouped bound supplies the cubic fallback even when the first pivot is uncertified.
11. **Computational claims are separated.** V3 only needs fixed-size OLS and pivot calculations. V1 confidence inversion and the V2 training optimizer are existence constructions.
12. **Sharp geometry is not sharp confidence calibration.** The full-radius comparison constants are conservative, and no exact optimal confidence-length constant is claimed.

## Next research target

Fix the target $A=e_2^{\otimes K}$ and consider unknown weak directions $v_j\perp\operatorname{span}(e_0,e_2)$ in $a e_0^{\otimes K}+b\bigotimes_jv_j$. Study one globally honest interval's worst expected length over these directions. A useful lower bound must separate the **target values of nearby alternatives**, not merely demonstrate that the $v_j$ cannot be estimated. The intermediate regime between $\sigma/\sqrt n$ and the existing dimension-dependent training threshold remains the main statistical gap.
