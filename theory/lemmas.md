# Essential lemmas and identities

The complete [numbered inventory](../audit/theorem_inventory.md) and [inline claim inventory](../audit/essential_claim_inventory.md) identify all statements and canonical proof locations.

1. Cap-one prefix ranks imply an exact product over consecutive blocks; internal ranks are preserved under external nonzero tensor products.
2. Orthogonal true degree-l perturbations are bounded by sqrt(C(J,l))(lambda+t)(t/lambda)^l.
3. Signed elementary TT-feasible paths isolate any loading degree by a Walsh average, even when smaller degrees are present.
4. Joint Gaussian KL is deltaᵀE(S)delta/(2sigma²); Pinsker and two-target inclusion give the honest-length lower bound.
5. The structural tube modulus lies between the maximum of the exact modulus and ||A||min(eta,t), and min(||A||t,eta||A||+omega_0(t+eta)).
6. Gaussian inverse moments follow from E(chi²_ν)^(-k/2)=2^(-k/2)Gamma((ν−k)/2)/Gamma(ν/2), requiring ν>k.
7. HOSVD residual≤sqrt(J)||Z−B0||; pilot error≤(1+sqrt(J))||Z−B0||.
8. Moving a true degree-l loading into a lower pilot degree r costs at least l−r factors of h/lambda.
9. The energy certificate uses e_l(x)≤C(J,l)(sum x/J)^l; its coefficient is sqrt(C(J,l))/J^(l/2).
10. With only m moments of localization, cap each degree at m when integrating the bad event. The minimum defining the certificate is not assumed subadditive.
11. Coordinate medians of g=2h−1 independent OLS groups satisfy P(||zmed−b||>u)≤min(1,(D/u)^(2h)); tail integration gives E||zmed−b||^r≤[2h/(2h−r)]D^r for r<2h.
12. Without a common known variance bound, design-dependent rare mixtures can change any regression coefficient while remaining arbitrarily close in total variation; C12 proves the corresponding expected-length obstruction.
