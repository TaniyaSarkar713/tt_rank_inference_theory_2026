# Candidate statements recorded before new experiments

Status at proposal stage: CONJECTURED pending complete derivation and checks. This historical file preserves the proposed claims; final status is in theorem_statements.md.

C1. At B0=lambda times a product of unit vectors, split the TT chain at exactly the cuts whose cap equals one. Decompose A by which resulting blocks are orthogonal to the signal. If the smallest nonzero block degree is k, effective contact order is q=max(1,k). Propose omega_A(t) comparable to t^q/lambda^(q-1), in fixed dimensions.

C2. For a unit elementary contrast active in q blocks, propose exact leading modulus coefficient q^(-q/2) lambda^(1-q). Arbitrary loadings may have more difficult constants.

C3. Over the entire capped-rank class, propose optimal pointwise honest expected length comparable to (sigma/sqrt(n))^q/lambda^(q-1). A complete proof must control expected length outside a pilot-localization event, not just coverage.

C4. For a known radius eta around the exact class, propose length comparable to min(eta,sigma/sqrt(n))+(sigma/sqrt(n))^q/lambda^(q-1). The generic tube sandwich is elementary; the TT classification is the substantive input.

C5. A pilot-centered interval using all orthogonal block orders, intersected with the ambient OLS confidence interval, should attain C3–C4. Prove conditional finite-sample coverage, and extend the OLS radius to unknown sigma using an F pivot.

C6. If every cut has room to increase its current rank by one, every nonzero loading has first-order modulus, regardless of regularity assumptions on the interval.

C7. For a three-block full-normal loading T, the exact cubic modulus coefficient should equal its tensor spectral norm divided by 3 sqrt(3) lambda². This would inherit known tensor-norm computational hardness when dimensions vary; it would not establish a computational-statistical minimax gap.

C8. Growing-dimension minimax expected length and general non-rank-one weak-bond transitions remain unresolved. Do not extrapolate fixed-dimensional constants into dimension-free rates.
