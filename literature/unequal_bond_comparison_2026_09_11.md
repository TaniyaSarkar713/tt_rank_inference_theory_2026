# Literature boundary for the unequal-bond extension

Checked 11 September 2026. This is a targeted comparison using the existing paper collection and a short search for TT singular-value geometry and rank-boundary inference. It is not a new exhaustive review or a novelty certificate.

The candidate addition is the all-radius product $t\prod_j\min(1,t/\beta_j)$ for fully normal loadings, with a feasible perturbation respecting every original TT cap. Its consequences include removal of the balanced-gap assumption and an explicit 23-feature interval for a quartic-to-cubic single-cut-loss family. The argument is a strengthening of the project's existing G1 bound and witness construction, rather than a new TT parametrization.

| [Budzinskiy and Zamarashkin, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.03975v3) | TT completion and local Riemannian convergence, with tangent-projector bounds involving a harmonic mean of unfolding singular values. | Metadata and abstract checked. Its full theorem-level comparison remains follow-up work. |

The direct searches did not expose a matching theorem in their returned primary results. That negative search observation is limited evidence; it does not establish that the clipped product is new. The strongest remaining novelty check is a specialist comparison of the full-radius construction with existing MPS/TT perturbation bounds.
