# Project origin and provenance

This is a new reassessment, not a relabeling of the earlier draft. The earlier project is ../jasa_tt_rank_inference and its bibliography/code are inputs. Only the Gaussian regression utility module and bibliography were copied. Earlier simulations are not pooled with the new experiments. The broader 18-paper folder review is ../research_review.

All PROVED labels in this project mean a complete derivation checked within this project. They do not certify first discovery, independent peer review, or formal verification.

The final provenance audit added lower/upper endpoints to each raw interval record and reexecuted the same deterministic streams. This enables direct reconstruction of coverage and length, rather than only reaggregation of saved indicators. No method, setting or random seed was changed, and these reruns are not counted as additional independent datasets. Execution hashes cover the sampler/method/runner dependencies; postprocessing scripts have separate project-level checksums.
